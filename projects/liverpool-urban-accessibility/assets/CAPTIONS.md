# Figure captions

## Local workplace-flow retention

`local-retention-map.png` maps the share of fixed-workplace flows from each of Liverpool's 61 Census
2021 MSOAs whose destination was within Liverpool, including the origin MSOA. The
denominator excludes the combined working-at-home or no-fixed-place category and other special
workplace locations. Values are descriptive of Census Day conditions and are not present-day demand.

Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0. Contains
OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026.

## Accessibility relationship

`accessibility-relationship.png` plots area local-retention share against gravity-style access to
Liverpool workplace destinations, using population-weighted centroid distances, destination inflow
mass and a 5 km exponential decay. Colour represents the combined working-at-home or no-fixed-place
share. Each point is one MSOA. The plot is descriptive; destination-flow mass comes from the same OD
release, excludes Scotland and Northern Ireland origins, and neither axis is an individual-level
measure.
