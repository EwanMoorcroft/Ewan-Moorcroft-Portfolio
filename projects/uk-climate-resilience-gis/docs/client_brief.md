# Decision brief: where to investigate adaptation needs

**To:** a simulated Greater Manchester adaptation programme team  
**From:** independent portfolio case study by Ewan Moorcroft  
**Evidence snapshot:** 29 September 2026; mixed source vintages documented separately  
**Decision sought:** agree an evidence-led programme of local investigations, with explicit review of model assumptions and missing evidence.

## Recommendation

Use the **153 weight-persistent neighbourhoods** as a starting set for joint local review. The balanced scenario initially flags 306 of 1,528 assessed LSOAs; the persistent set remains in the top group under all three policy scenarios and at least 80% of the sampled weights. Group neighbouring areas into practicable investigations after examining their component evidence and local context.

Run an evidence-improvement track alongside that work for the **174 unassessed LSOAs**, which contain 290,082 Census residents. Their absence from the shortlist does not show low need. Heat gaps are uneven: Salford has evidence for 119 of 161 LSOAs, compared with 192 of 200 in Wigan. The [borough table](../outputs/tables/borough_summary.csv) supplies both denominators and excluded counts.

Before using the list to allocate investigative capacity, agree whether the programme values **relative disadvantage across dimensions** (the midrank reference model) or **raw differences in indicator magnitude** (the min–max alternative). Their balanced top-group overlap is only 0.464 by Jaccard. This is a material policy choice, not a technical detail that can be hidden behind the weight-persistence flag.

## Translate screening into work

| Workstream | Evidence supplied | Next professional task | Decision it could inform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flood resilience | Exact scenario overlaps and alternative footprint | Review local flood models, buildings/assets, drainage and defence assumptions with specialists | Whether a location warrants a site-scale flood study |
| Heat and cooling | Selected-date surface anomalies, observation counts and coverage | Check indoor/air-temperature evidence, shade, materials and affected facilities | Where to monitor and assess cooling options |
| Greenspace access | Official household deficit plus a separate network case | Audit entrances, missing paths, crossings, opening hours and accessible routes | Whether the constraint is access, quality, provision or incomplete map data |
| Inclusive adaptation | Income and aggregate age context | Engage residents and services without inferring individual circumstances | Design and prioritise appropriate engagement and support |
| Evidence gaps | Explicit unassessed areas and reasons | Acquire/validate additional thermal or local hazard evidence | Whether a currently unranked area should enter the comparison |

In the case study, 498,833 residents are represented by centroids with a finite mapped route longer than 1 km. Another 18,941 residents are represented by 54 disconnected origins. Treating those unresolved origins as all accessible or all inaccessible produces a **60.7%–63.0% scenario range**, not a confidence interval. Those origins are an actionable first network-data audit. The destination definition is public parks/gardens ≥10 ha, not every green space.

## Guardrails for interpretation

The persistent set contains 257,388 Census residents, not 257,388 people predicted to be harmed or helped. Scores are relative to the assessed region; they do not estimate damage, health burden or benefit-cost ratios. The flood footprint is not population exposure. The selected thermal dates are not climate projections. Defra household-access rates and case resident-access rates are different measures.

The observed global spatial association supports inspecting neighbourhood context and adjoining areas together. It does not establish a causal intervention mechanism. Local feasibility, resident priorities, costs, land ownership, infrastructure and more complete hazard evidence are required before investment decisions. This case study supplies the analytical handover for those next steps; it does not claim they have happened.
